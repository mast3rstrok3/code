# Organizing threads

Pin a thread from its context menu to keep it in the pinned section above your active work.
Pinned threads are shown independently of their project, including when you connect to more than
one environment.

On web and desktop, drag a pinned thread to change its position. On mobile, open the thread's menu
and choose **Move up** or **Move down**. The order is stored by the server and appears on your
other connected devices.

If reordering is unavailable for one environment, update the T3 Code server running in that
environment. Older servers can still pin and unpin threads, but do not understand synced ordering;
their pinned threads keep the default newest-first order below the ones you have arranged.

## Thread actions

On web and desktop, hover over an active thread and choose **Settle**, or right-click a thread to
open its full action menu. Settling moves finished work into the receded history section without
deleting its conversation. Settled threads offer **Un-settle**, and snoozed threads offer **Wake**.

In the web app on a touch device, swipe a thread left to reveal its lifecycle action. A full swipe
runs it immediately: **Settle** for active or pinned threads, **Un-settle** for settled threads, and
**Wake** for snoozed threads. Long-press any thread to open the full action menu. Choose **Delete**
there to permanently remove the thread after confirmation.
